Ring me when the app is closed

Your phone shows a real notification for calls and messages even when To you is closed. No extra app needed. It needs a free relay, one person sets it up (see PUSH-SETUP.md) and everyone uses the same address.

iPhone: first tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open To you from there.

Other way: ntfy app

Without this, the other person's phone only rings while To you is open. To get a real ring even when it is closed, install the free ntfy app (Play Store or App Store) and follow these steps.

  1. Install ntfy and open it.
  2. Tap +, paste this topic name exactly, and subscribe:
turn it on first

3. In your phone settings, allow ntfy notifications and sound. Then tap a "calling" notification to open the call.

Privacy: this tells the free ntfy server that someone is calling you (their name and the time). Chats and calls stay locked.

Calls that will not connect

If you set the relay address above, To you gets a free call relay from it automatically. Turn this on if calls still fail on one network.

Some phone networks block direct calls. A relay passes the call along. It still cannot hear it, the call stays encrypted. The free public one is not always reliable.

Make a free account at metered.ca (20 GB of relay a month). Open Dashboard, then TURN Server, and copy your app name (the part before .metered.live) and the API key. Paste them here. Only one of you needs this.
